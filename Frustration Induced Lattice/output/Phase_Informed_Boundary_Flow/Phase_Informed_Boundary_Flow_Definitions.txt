PHASE-INFORMED RELAY-COMPATIBLE BOUNDARY FLOW

Basic HDF5 data
---------------
X_i^n = (x_i^n,y_i^n): row i of positionX at saved frame n.
theta_i^n: row i of phaseTheta at saved frame n.
Delta t_s = dt * shotsnaps: time between saved frames.
u_i^n = (cos(theta_i^n), sin(theta_i^n)): phase-defined heading.

Boundary coordinates and phase support
--------------------------------------
Pi(X_i^n) = (d_i^n,s_i^n,t_i^n,kappa_(b,i)^n,e_i^n) is the unique regular
closest-boundary projection.  s increases counterclockwise, t is its unit
tangent, d is wall distance, kappa_b is signed boundary curvature, and e is
the regular boundary component.  Non-unique projections are invalid.  For
polygonal and arc-line boundaries, the regularity mask also excludes an
arclength neighborhood 0.05 D0 on every incident
component next to a vertex/join.  The separate distance-tie tolerance is
1e-07 D0; on a circle this excludes only the center
up to that tolerance.

q_i^n = u_i^n dot t_i^n.
Delta s_i^n = wrap_P(s_i^(n+1)-s_i^n) in [-P/2,P/2).

For component e of length L_e, set

N_e = ceil[L_e / (0.5 D0)]

and divide it into N_e equal cells of exact length ell_e=L_e/N_e.  Thus no cell
crosses a vertex or arc-line join, and the exact ell_k are retained in every
geometric sum.

At each frame n and cell k, choose the smallest-d regular candidate satisfying
|q| >= q0 = 0.05.  For 0<alpha<pi/2, candidates
come from the complete unique regular nearest-boundary coordinate domain; no
fixed radial cutoff is imposed because this regime has a broad boundary-flow
layer.  For pi/2<alpha<pi, candidates additionally require d<=D0 and the W
condition below.  q0 is a pre-registered directional-resolution dead band: it
is not a tangential-dominance criterion and is never used as a current weight.
Let Q_nk=sign(q) for the nearest representative.  If the candidate set is empty,
Q_nk=0.  An opposite-sign tie within the registered distance tolerance also
gives Q_nk=0.

The main table uses q0=0.05.  Robustness is
reported separately for q0 in (0.02, 0.05, 0.1); sensitivity values never
replace or enter the two headline observables.

High-alpha phase/curvature filter (alpha > pi/2 only)
-----------------------------------------------------
N_i^n = {j != i : ||X_j^n-X_i^n|| <= D0}.

omega_i^n = omega_i + K * [
    mean_(j in N_i^n) sin(theta_j^n-theta_i^n+alpha) - sin(alpha)
].

If N_i^n is empty, the bracketed coupling is zero, exactly as in main.py.
This omega is recomputed from each saved (positionX,phaseTheta) state; it is
not a finite difference of phaseTheta.

kappa_d,i^n = kappa_b,i^n / (1-kappa_b,i^n d_i^n).
W_i^n = kappa_d,i^n v (q_i^n)^2 - omega_i^n q_i^n.

For alpha > pi/2, a frame candidate must additionally satisfy

d_i^n <= D0,   1-kappa_(b,i)^n d_i^n > 0,   W_i^n > epsilon_W,

where
epsilon_W = 0.02 v/D0.
W is a candidate filter, not a third observable.  It selects the conservative
free-turning-deficit branch; W<=epsilon_W is not asserted to classify all Bulk
motion, and W is not itself a topology test.

Direction-neutral kinematic calibration (not a headline metric)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Track only frame n's gate representative to n+1.  A calibration trial T_nk=1
requires regular same-component projections at both endpoints, the regime's
spatial/W conditions, |q| >= q0, equal endpoint phase signs, and a unique
geometry-bounded wrapped increment.  A success E_nk=1 additionally requires

|Delta s|/(v Delta t_s) >= 0.02

and sign(Delta s)=Q_nk.  Positive and negative trials are pooled: no majority
direction is learned or imposed.  Gate k is calibrated only when

sum_n T_nk >= 3

and

C_k = sum_n E_nk / sum_n T_nk >=
0.66666667.

Calibration counts and C_k are quality control only; they never multiply either
headline observable.  Insufficient calibration is reported as unavailable,
not as zero transport.

Two-frame phase support and short-block coherence
-------------------------------------------------
The minimum non-instantaneous Eulerian support is

Y_nk = Q_nk when Q_nk=Q_(n+1)k != 0; otherwise Y_nk=0.

The two representatives may have different particle identities, which permits
relay transport.  The target block time is

tau_target = 1 D0/v.

For saved spacing Delta t_s the actual integer block is

m = ceil(tau_target/Delta t_s),   Delta T_b = m Delta t_s.

For the analyzed files m=7 and Delta T_b=0.35 (tau_target=1/3).  Within block b
define

N_bk^+ = #{n in b : Y_nk=+1},
N_bk^- = #{n in b : Y_nk=-1},
p_bk = |N_bk^+-N_bk^-|/(N_bk^++N_bk^-).

Z_bk = sign(N_bk^+-N_bk^-) only when N_bk^++N_bk^->0,
p_bk >= 0.66666667, and gate k is calibrated;
otherwise Z_bk=0.  Counts decide only whether the observed directions conflict;
they do not weight any later sum.

Spatial validity gate (not a headline metric)
----------------------------------------------
Let A_b={k:Z_bk != 0}.  The union over the complete terminal observation
window is valid only when

max_(C in Conn(union_b A_b)) Length(C) >=
2 D0.

Length means the largest contiguous periodic arclength component; disconnected
gate fragments are never added together.  Adjacent component-end cells remain
adjacent along the physical closed boundary, so a macroscopic arc may pass a
zero-measure vertex/join even though no individual cell or calibration interval
crosses that join.  The reported extent consequently has gate-scale and
join-exclusion-scale discretization uncertainty.

The only two headline observables
---------------------------------
M_b = 1 when the spatial validity gate passes and A_b is nonempty; otherwise 0.
If the calibration or spatial validity gate is insufficient, the state is NA,
not zero.

Xi_Persist = (1/B) sum_b M_b.

With physical gate lengths ell_k,

chi_b = [sum_k ell_k Z_bk] / [sum_k ell_k |Z_bk|] in [-1,1]

for active blocks, including chi_b=0 ties, and

Xi_Sign = | sum_b M_b chi_b / sum_b M_b |.

Xi_Sign is NA when fewer than 3 blocks
are active.  Neither headline observable is multiplied by carrier fraction,
particle count, event count, crossing rate, or current strength.  Different
particles may therefore supply consecutive Eulerian phase supports.  The result
is consistent with relay transport but does not reconstruct a unique causal
relay trajectory.

Interpretation protocol
-----------------------
The alpha=pi/2 Pattern-Formation boundary is an experimental prior, not an
output of these observables.  alpha=0 is a singular endpoint and alpha=pi is a
separate bidirectional boundary-lattice-flow problem; both endpoints are
excluded from the two single-handed observables and reported as NA.
