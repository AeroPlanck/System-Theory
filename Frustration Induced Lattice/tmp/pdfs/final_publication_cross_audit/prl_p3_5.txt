in simulation length units (h–n), for L = 7, N = 2000, K = 20.75, v = 3, and d0 = 1; color gives θ. Columns sweep
α = 0, 0.2π, 0.4π, 0.5π, 0.6π, 0.8π, π. The associated trajectory analysis assigns no persistent single chirality or topological
protection at α = 0. For 0 < α < π/2, an all-particle chiral stream forms without an interior pattern but is readily disrupted
by the isolated defect. At α = π/2, the unidirectional boundary lattice has spacing of order d0 and remains defect-sensitive.
For π/2 < α < π, the interior vortex lattice supports a highly coherent minority edge stream that survives the defect. At the
singular phase-lag endpoint α = π, trajectory data show two counterpropagating boundary-lattice streams whose spacing is
associated with the limiting ﬁnite-wavenumber scale 2π/k∗ rather than the threshold scale d0 ; this bidirectional state is distinct
from the chiral α = π/2 lattice and carries no Chern label within the continuum closure.
